# Sample: Hello / مرحبا

5 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. A sample that shows how a video is laid out in this kit and what a drawn
look is made of (the style toolkit in `src/style`): replace it.

Look: cel-shaded rooftops at dusk, ink edges, painted walls and sky, paper grain.

The data the code and the scripts read is `storyboard.json`. Keep the two files in step.

## Shot 1: Rooftops at dusk, Hello (frames 0–74, 2.5 s)

Rooftops at dusk under a painted sky: painted walls, lit windows, water tanks, antennas, a laundry line.
The camera slides slowly to the right. The word **Hello** eases in at the centre.

- Text: "Hello", frames 12–74.
- Review frames: 0, 20, 45, 74.
- Checks:
  1. Hello is sharp, centred and readable against the sky.
  2. Nothing overlaps the word Hello: every roof, antenna and pole stays below it.
  3. No building is a plain box: each has a painted wall, and those facing us have windows.
  4. The near roof carries props (tanks, air conditioners, pots, a laundry line); it is not empty.

## Shot 2: Hello steps back, مرحبا arrives (frames 75–149, 2.5 s)

Hello moves up and shrinks. The Arabic word **مرحبا** rises into the centre and becomes the focal point.
The rooftops keep sliding slowly.

- Text: "Hello", frames 75–149; "مرحبا", frames 84–149.
- Review frames: 75, 95, 120, 149.
- Checks:
  1. The Arabic letters are joined and the word reads right to left.
  2. By frame 120 the Arabic word is the largest thing on screen and centred.
  3. Hello stays readable above it and does not touch it.
  4. The last frame is calm: nothing is still arriving, and the picture still reads as drawn (ink lines,
     painted surfaces, grain).
